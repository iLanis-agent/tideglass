# Tideglass 1.0 TEST
Native Java/Canvas Android 8+ Aquarium water-level puzzle.

Fill each outlined aquarium with water from the bottom upward, or leave it empty. Every cell of the same tank in the same row has the same wet or dry state, even across separated arms. If a tank cell is wet, every cell of that tank in lower rows is wet too. Different tanks may have different levels. Gold numbers above and beside the grid give exact water-cell totals in each column and row. Tap a dry cell to fill its whole tank up to that row. Tap a wet cell to drain that row and every higher row of its tank. Twelve distinct 4x4 room/count boards have twelve distinct unique solutions.

Saved water palette and room labels, GG007 icon/menu/site/privacy, unlimited undo/reset. Offline, no ads, analytics, account or requested permissions. Settings and completed-puzzle marks stay on device. External studio link opens browser.

Independent Java oracle enumerates the Cartesian product of all flat tank cutoff levels and then counts row/column cells. It agrees with production pairwise same-tank same-or-lower-row implications and line sums on all 65536 masks per puzzle. Exactly one solution per board; bounds, every-cell fill/drain, solve, undo and replay pass. Adapter navigation, saved settings, privacy, taps, next, levels and persistence pass. Actual drawing pixels inspected, not phone screenshots. Phone runtime, performance and browser handoff QA incomplete. Signed TEST only, not final or Play published.

Current 803-entry registry, 130 partial recovered README records and exact Candyreed cumulative stage found no Aquarium or flat-tank water-level match. Unlike Fallbriar/Stostone, the tanks stay fixed and water uses flat cutoffs instead of dropping rigid connected stones. Unlike Cloudball/Dosun-Fuwari, there are no balloon/iron pieces or local support constraints. Row and column water totals constrain whole-tank fill/drain actions, not independent tile shading. Known Aquarium genre, not a global invention. Partial corpus, not ALL-local. This implements the single-flat-level rule, including split tank arms, not a physics-only variation. Rules: https://www.puzzle-aquarium.com/ and https://wpcunofficial.miraheze.org/wiki/Aquarium .
